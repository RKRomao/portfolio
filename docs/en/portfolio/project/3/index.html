NeoLAAC

11/06/2026

NeoLAAC (Linha de Apoio ao Caloiro) is a digital platform designed by and for students at the Universidade da Beira Interior (UBI). Its primary goal is to act as a "digital mentor," simplifying the transition for incoming freshmen by centralizing academic, logistical, and social resources to reduce anxiety and disorientation.

Detailed Description:

Overview and Purpose

NeoLAAC is a comprehensive digital ecosystem designed to support the integration of incoming students (freshmen) at Universidade da Beira Interior (UBI). Acting as a "digital mentor", it unifies academic, logistical, and social resources into a mobile-first application.

System Architecture

Built on a robust, scalable microservices architecture:

  • API Gateway/Orchestrator (laac-orq): Central routing, security, and auditing.
  • Docker Containerization: Isolated services allowing seamless horizontal scaling.
  • Database-per-Service: Isolated MariaDB instances ensuring high concurrency resilience.

Core Modules & Features

1. Spatial Orientation (map-service)

  • Interactive Campus Map: GeoJSON maps detailing faculties, building blocks, and classrooms.
  • Pedestrian Navigation: Step-by-step GPS routing to specific classrooms.
  • Urban Mobility: Public transit schedules and routes in Covilhã.

2. Hybrid Support & Emergency (chatbot-service, emergency-service)

  • Digital Mentor (Chatbot): 24/7 natural language AI assistant.
  • Intelligent Escalation: Automatic transfer to human staff upon detecting critical emergency keywords.
  • Panic Button & Support Tickets: Immediate priority alerts and incident tracking.

3. Academic Life & Logistics (academic-service, calendar-service)

  • Academic Calendar & Schedules: Class times, rooms, and exam sync.
  • Error Reporting: Ticket submission for schedule corrections.

4. Community & Social (feed-service, events-service)

  • Unified Feed & Student Organizations: Institutional news and student group event management.
  • Survival Guides & Perks: Student survival guides and local business discount directory.

Security & Quality

  • Security: Encrypted credentials, JWT authentication, and RBAC permissions.
  • Resilience & Performance: Daily automated backups, /health checks, and API latency under 200ms.
